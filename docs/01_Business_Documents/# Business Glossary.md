# Business Glossary

## Purpose

This document defines the key business terms used throughout the Health Insurance Data Warehouse project. It provides a common business vocabulary for data engineers, analysts, and report developers working with the Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.

| Business Term             | Definition                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Policy**                | An insurance contract issued by the insurer that defines the coverage provided to one or more insured individuals.    |
| **Policy ID**             | Unique identifier assigned to an insurance policy. Multiple insured individuals can belong to the same policy.        |
| **Insured**               | An individual covered under an insurance policy and eligible to receive healthcare benefits.                          |
| **Insured ID**            | Identifier representing an insured individual's position within a policy.                                             |
| **Coverage Period**       | The time interval during which an insured individual or policy remains active.                                        |
| **Policy Effective Date** | Date on which a policy becomes active.                                                                                |
| **Policy Lapse Date**     | Date on which a policy terminates. A NULL value indicates the policy is still active.                                 |
| **Exposure Time**         | Fraction of the calendar year during which an insured individual was covered by the policy.                           |
| **Premium**               | Annual insurance amount paid by the insured for policy coverage.                                                      |
| **Claim**                 | A request for reimbursement or payment for healthcare services covered under the policy.                              |
| **Claim Cost**            | Total monetary value of claims generated by an insured individual during the observation period.                      |
| **Medical Service**       | Any healthcare consultation, treatment, procedure, diagnostic test, or medical activity utilized by the insured.      |
| **Policy Lapse**          | Termination of an insurance policy before or at the end of the policy period.                                         |
| **New Business**          | A policy issued to a newly acquired customer during the reporting period rather than a renewal.                       |
| **Policy Renewal**        | Continuation of an existing insurance policy into a new coverage period.                                              |
| **Customer Tenure**       | Total duration for which an insured individual has remained continuously covered by the insurer.                      |
| **Policy Tenure**         | Number of years since a policy was originally issued, irrespective of changes in insured members.                     |
| **Individual Policy**     | Policy covering a single individual or family purchased independently.                                                |
| **Collective Policy**     | Policy issued under a group contract such as an employer or organization.                                             |
| **Insurance Product**     | Coverage plan offered by the insurer, such as Standard, Premium, Dental, or International.                            |
| **Reimbursement**         | Product feature indicating whether eligible medical expenses incurred outside the provider network can be reimbursed. |
| **Distribution Channel**  | Sales channel through which the insurance policy was purchased, such as Agency, Direct, or Insurance Intermediary.    |
| **Income Percentile**     | Socioeconomic indicator representing the relative income level of the insured's geographical area.                    |
| **Habitat Size**          | Classification of a municipality based on its population size.                                                        |
| **Climate Cluster**       | Geographic classification grouping regions with similar climatic characteristics.                                     |
| **Bronze Layer**          | Raw ingestion layer that stores source data with minimal transformation while preserving lineage.                     |
| **Silver Layer**          | Cleansed and standardized layer containing validated and integrated business entities.                                |
| **Gold Layer**            | Business-ready dimensional model optimized for reporting, analytics, and machine learning workloads.                  |
| **Dimension Table**       | Descriptive table containing business attributes used to provide analytical context to fact tables.                   |
| **Fact Table**            | Table containing measurable business events and numerical metrics used for reporting and analysis.                    |
| **Data Lineage**          | End-to-end traceability of data movement from the source dataset through Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.             |
| **ETL Pipeline**          | Data engineering workflow responsible for extracting, transforming, validating, and loading data into the warehouse.  |

---

## Scope

This glossary applies to all business entities, datasets, ETL pipelines, dimensional models, dashboards, and analytical solutions developed as part of the Health Insurance Data Warehouse project.
