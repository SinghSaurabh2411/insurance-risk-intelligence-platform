# Business Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary preserves the business terminology collected for the Insurance Risk Intelligence Platform. It is a business-reference document, not a substitute for the implemented schema. The committed SQL DDL and Python transformations define the actual warehouse objects, columns and grains.

Definitions below are retained from the project's original glossary. They are not independently verified by the ETL code. Where a definition implies a business rule (for example, how NULL lapse dates are interpreted), confirm it against the source dataset documentation or an approved business definition before using it as an authoritative RAG answer or analytical rule.

| Business term | Definition recorded in the project glossary |
|---|---|
| Policy | An insurance contract issued by the insurer that defines the coverage provided to one or more insured individuals. |
| Policy ID | Identifier assigned to an insurance policy. Multiple insured individuals can belong to the same policy. |
| Insured | An individual covered under an insurance policy and eligible to receive healthcare benefits. |
| Insured ID | Identifier representing an insured individual's position within a policy. |
| Coverage Period | The time interval during which an insured individual or policy remains active. |
| Policy Effective Date | Date on which a policy becomes active. |
| Policy Lapse Date | Date on which a policy terminates. The original glossary states that NULL indicates the policy is still active; verify this interpretation against source documentation. |
| Exposure Time | Described in the original glossary as the fraction of the calendar year during which an insured individual was covered. The unit/scale must be confirmed from source documentation. |
| Premium | Described in the original glossary as the annual insurance amount paid by the insured for policy coverage. Confirm the precise source definition and aggregation semantics before treating it as authoritative. |
| Claim | A request for reimbursement or payment for healthcare services covered under the policy. This is a business term only; the current warehouse does not implement a claim-level entity or fact. |
| Claim Cost | Described in the original glossary as the total monetary value of claims generated by an insured individual during the observation period. The implemented field is `COST_CLAIMS_YEAR` in `SILVER_FINANCIAL` and `FACT_POLICY`; no separate claim-level grain is implemented. |
| Medical Service | The original glossary describes healthcare consultations, treatment, procedures, diagnostic tests or other medical activity utilized by the insured. |
| Policy Lapse | Termination of an insurance policy before or at the end of the policy period. |
| New Business | The original glossary describes a policy issued to a newly acquired customer during the reporting period rather than a renewal. |
| Policy Renewal | Continuation of an existing insurance policy into a new coverage period. |
| Customer Tenure | Duration for which an insured individual has remained continuously covered by the insurer. |
| Policy Tenure | Number of years since a policy was originally issued, irrespective of changes in insured members. |
| Individual Policy | Policy covering a single individual or family purchased independently. |
| Collective Policy | Policy issued under a group contract such as an employer or organization. |
| Insurance Product | Coverage plan offered by the insurer. The original examples include Standard, Premium, Dental and International; verify categories against source values. |
| Reimbursement | The original glossary describes a product feature indicating whether eligible medical expenses incurred outside the provider network can be reimbursed. |
| Distribution Channel | Sales channel through which the insurance policy was purchased. The original examples include Agency, Direct and Insurance Intermediary; verify categories against source values. |
| Income Percentile | Socioeconomic indicator representing the relative income level of the insured's geographical area. |
| Habitat Size | Classification of a municipality based on its population size. |
| Climate Cluster | Geographic classification grouping regions with similar climatic characteristics. |
| Bronze Layer | Raw ingestion layer storing source data with technical transformations and lineage fields. |
| Silver Layer | Validated subject-oriented projections of source observations. |
| Gold Layer | Dimensional model used by the current analytics API and dashboard. |
| Dimension Table | Descriptive table containing business attributes used to provide analytical context to fact tables. |
| Fact Table | Table containing measurable observations and numerical metrics. The implemented fact is `FACT_POLICY` at `(ID_POLICY, ID_INSURED, PERIOD)` grain. |
| Data Lineage | Traceability fields carried through the warehouse, including load/source and record-hash metadata. |
| ETL Pipeline | Workflow responsible for extracting, transforming, validating and loading data into the warehouse. |

## Implemented model terminology

- Silver has eight business tables: `SILVER_RECORD`, `SILVER_TIME`, `SILVER_POLICY`, `SILVER_CUSTOMER`, `SILVER_PRODUCT`, `SILVER_CHANNEL`, `SILVER_COVERAGE`, and `SILVER_FINANCIAL`.
- `SILVER_TIME` contains `PERIOD` only.
- Policy lifecycle year fields are in `SILVER_POLICY`; insured lifecycle year fields are in `SILVER_CUSTOMER`.
- `COST_CLAIMS_YEAR` is a financial measure; there is no `SILVER_CLAIMS` or `FACT_CLAIMS`.
- Gold consists of `DIM_TIME`, `DIM_CUSTOMER`, `DIM_POLICY`, `DIM_PRODUCT`, `DIM_CHANNEL`, and `FACT_POLICY`.

For exact columns and keys, use the DDL and `docs/02_Data_Architecture/Data Model.md`. Do not infer column definitions or business semantics from glossary wording alone.
